1. 写作结构：
	结构：四段式，开始借用题目句引出，随后 Primarily，Moreover，Ultimately 三段论。
		开头：背景引入 + 点出竞争来源 + 表明大学生需要做好准备。
		Primarily：写心理挑战，如 anxiety、digital saturation、social media comparison。
		Moreover：写就业挑战，如 AI-induced job displacement、traditional jobs being replaced。
		Ultimately：总结应对方法，强调把挑战转化为成长机会。

4. 段落积累
```
In Conclusion, AI and robotics are transforming the job market. It is high time that both governments and individuals took action to minimize the risks and turn this potential challenge into an opportunity for growth.

Knowledge acquired on campus may quickly become obsolete, yet the habit of lifelong learning empowers students to stay competitive in a fluid job market.

Rather than passively awaiting opportunities, students should proactively cultivate new competences, expand their intellectual horizons and align themselves with emerging trends.

In an era of relentless change, the capacity for continuous learning has emerged as an indispensable attribute for college students.

In an era of rapid transformation, many are eager to forge their own paths, yet they often clutch onto outdated traditional ideas for security. We must realize that change is embedded in the very nature of progress. Only by letting go of our fear and embracing innovation can we truly thrive.

It is incumbent upon to the younger generation to synthesize existing knowledge with innovative thinking to address the pressing issues of our time.

In today's fiercely competitive society, young people should not only acquire professional knowledge, but also develop a strong sense of responsibility and the courage to face difficulties.
```

   5. 模拟写作

```
In recent years, college students’ mental health has emerged as an increasingly pressing concern. Many students suffer from anxiety, depression, or even lose interest in study.

Several factors contribute to this phenomenon. First, academic pressure is the dominant factor. Students constantly worry about exams, grades and scholarships. Second, a lack of interpersonal communication can also be a source of stress. Some college students stay in their comfort zones and are unwilling to expand their social circles. If these challenges are not properly addressed, they may suffer from more serious mental problems.

A multi-faceted approach can solve the above problems. On the one hand, actively seeking help is the first step to improve the situation. Rather than passively awaiting assistance, students should consult teachers or friends on their own initiative. On the other hand, moderate physical exercise can liberate students from increasingly intense pressure. It is high time that college students took action to turn this pressure into motivation.

In conclusion, mental health is of great value to college students. Only by managing pressure properly and embracing wholesome lifestyles can we truly thrive.
```

Nowadays, college students are increasingly encouraged to step beyond the campus and engage with society. This tendency is of great significance, for education should not be confined to textbooks or classrooms. What students acquire from lectures may provide them with theoretical knowledge, but social practice enables them to test, enrich and apply that knowledge in real-life situations.

Through social practice, students can gain a deeper understanding of how society actually operates. For instance, by taking part in volunteer work, community research or internships, they may become more aware of ordinary people’s needs and the complexity of real-world problems. Such experiences can cultivate their empathy, strengthen their communication skills and help them develop a stronger sense of responsibility.

More importantly, social practice allows college students to discover their own strengths and limitations. In the process of cooperating with others and solving practical problems, they gradually learn what kind of person they want to become and what contribution they can make to society. Therefore, social practice is not merely an addition to academic learning; it is an essential path through which students grow into mature, responsible and capable individuals.
